Privacy Policy — Mente Curiosa

Last updated: October 31, 2026.

About this app

Mente Curiosa is a personal app. It lets its owner connect their own TikTok account in order to publish their own videos. The app is for the owner's personal use only.

Data collected

The app collects only the minimum data needed to sign in with TikTok and to publish videos. This includes the TikTok account identifier and the authorization tokens needed for sign-in and publishing, and the video files the owner chooses to upload. The app does not collect any other personal data.

How data is used

Collected data is used only to sign in to TikTok and to publish the owner's videos. It is not used for any other purpose.

Sharing

The app does not sell data. Data is not shared with any third party, except with TikTok through its API, only as necessary to sign in and publish videos.

Storage and security

Authorization tokens are kept private by the owner and are not shared with anyone else.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The version published on this page is the current version.
